HOW TO PLAY

On Phone

  • Tap the arrow buttons on the left and right edges to move.
  • Tap the fullscreen icon to expand the game.

On Laptop

  • Click the game screen to start.
  • Use the left and right arrow keys (← →) to move.
  • Press the X key to start or confirm.
  • Click the fullscreen icon to expand the game.

Psst... you get 3 misses before the game ends. Good luck!

PRO TIP Go fullscreen for the full experience. Look for the glowing fullscreen icon in the top-left corner after you start.
