HOW TO PLAY

On Phone

  • Tap the arrow buttons on the left and right edges to move.
  • Or tap the left or right half of the screen.
  • Tap the ⛶ icon to go fullscreen (recommended).

On Laptop

  • Click into the game once, then use ← → to move.
  • Press X to start or confirm.
  • Click the ⛶ icon for fullscreen.

Psst... you get 3 misses before the game ends. Good luck!

PRO TIP Go fullscreen for the full experience. Look for the glowing ⛶ in the top-left after you start.
